1 · NeurReps 2026 — Extended Abstract #46 (this poster)

Three guards for subspace claims: chance, stability and metric

Question. Do evoked neural population patterns live inside the spontaneous-activity subspace? Classics said yes; modern large-scale recordings say near-orthogonal. The field’s standard “containment” number is not one well-posed quantity.

Three guards. Report excess over an explicit rotation null — a random k-dim subspace already captures k/n of any probe (a 10–30-point floor at classic scales). Screen conditioning by the boundary gap σk/σk+1 — median gaps fall below 1.05 beyond k ≈ 3–5, while published work uses k = 10–128. Declare the metric — rate normalisation moves hippocampal cross-state excess from 0.32–0.61 to 0.02–0.19.

Result. The literature’s disagreement decomposes into chance-floor inflation, ill-conditioned projectors and undeclared metric + state mixing; the small V1 excess survives all guards. Data: 60 Neuropixels populations, ~10k-neuron two-photon V1, monkey DMFC, HD thalamus, hippocampal CA1; measures ship with a 15-test known-answer battery.

2 · RECOMB 2027 — Submitted (EasyChair #22)

Separating higher-order information from prediction in cancer co-mutation

Most “higher-order” claims in biology rest on pairwise statistics. This work asks how much of cancer co-mutation structure is genuinely beyond pairs.

It separates two quantities that are often conflated: the higher-order information present in the data versus the predictive gain a model can actually extract from it.

Solo-authored; code at github.com/freemanhui/beyond-pairs.

3 · C. elegans — Single-cell programme

A worm virtual cell: atlas, state models & a small-RNA study

C. elegans is the only animal with a complete cell lineage and a complete connectome (302 neurons) — a whole-organism testbed the large labs leave alone.

Atlas direction: a worm-specific embedding and an open cell-type annotation tool on Arc scBaseCount data (295,473 cells, 58 samples), with a daf-2 ageing-perturbation case study under strict held-out validation — baselines first, per the Virtual Cell Challenge lesson.

Companion paper: “The inheritance state reweights the small RNA pool beyond uniform gain in C. elegans” — manuscript in submission at Briefings in Functional Genomics (Oxford University Press).
